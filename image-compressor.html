How to compress an image

  1. Tap "Choose an image" and select a photo from your phone.
  2. Move the quality slider. Lower quality gives a smaller file.
  3. Tap "Compress Image" and check the new size.
  4. Tap "Download Compressed Image" to save it.

Why compress images?

Smaller images upload faster, take less storage and make websites load quicker. They are also easier to send on email and WhatsApp.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this image compressor free?

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. Compression happens inside your browser, so your photos stay private.

Which format is the output?

The compressed image is saved as a JPG file. Transparent areas of PNG images become white.
