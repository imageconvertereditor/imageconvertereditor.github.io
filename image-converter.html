How to convert an image

  1. Tap "Choose an image" and select a photo from your phone.
  2. Pick the format you want: JPG, PNG or WebP.
  3. Tap "Convert Image".
  4. Tap "Download Converted Image" to save the new file.

Which format should I choose?

  • JPG is best for photos. Files are small and work everywhere.
  • PNG keeps full quality and supports transparent backgrounds. It is good for logos and screenshots.
  • WebP makes small files with good quality and is great for websites.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this image converter free?

Yes, it is completely free with no signup and no watermark.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. The conversion happens inside your browser, so your photos stay private.

What happens to transparent backgrounds?

PNG and WebP keep transparency. JPG cannot store it, so transparent areas become white.

Why is WebP not available on my device?

A few older browsers cannot create WebP files. If that happens, choose JPG or PNG instead.
