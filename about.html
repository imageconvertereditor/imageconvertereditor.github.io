About Us

Welcome to Image Converter & Editor. Our goal is to give everyone simple, fast and free online tools to work with images, without any signup and without installing an app.

What we offer

  • Image Compressor to reduce the size of your photos.
  • Image Resizer to change the width and height of an image.
  • JPG to PNG and PNG to JPG converters.
  • Image Cropper to cut out the part of a photo you need.
  • Image Converter to switch between JPG, PNG and WebP.

Your privacy comes first

All our image tools run inside your own browser. When you choose a photo, it is processed on your device and is not uploaded to our servers.

Free to use

Our tools are free to use. To cover the cost of running the website, we may show advertisements on our pages.
