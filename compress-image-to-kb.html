How to compress an image to a specific size

  1. Tap "Choose an image" and select your photo.
  2. Type the size you need in KB, or tap 20, 50, 100 or 200 KB.
  3. Tap "Compress Image". The tool lowers the quality and, if needed, the pixel size until the file fits.
  4. Check the new size and tap "Download Compressed Image".

Tips for form photos

  • Check the size limit on the form and enter a number a little smaller, for example 95 KB for a 100 KB limit.
  • Very small limits like 20 KB make the photo smaller and softer. This is normal.
  • Some forms also need an exact width and height. Use the Image Resizer or the Image Cropper first.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this tool free?

Yes, it is completely free with no signup and no watermark.

Are my photos uploaded to a server?

No. Everything happens inside your browser, so your photos stay private.

What if the tool cannot reach my target size?

Very large photos or very small targets can be hard to reach. The tool shows the closest size it could get. Try a slightly higher target or crop the photo first.

What format is the result?

The result is a JPG file. Transparent areas of PNG images become white.
