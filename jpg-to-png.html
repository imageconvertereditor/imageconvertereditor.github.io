JPG vs PNG

JPG files are smaller and good for photos. PNG files keep every detail without losing quality and support transparent backgrounds, which makes them useful for logos, screenshots and graphics. Converting a JPG to PNG does not add new detail, but it saves the image in a lossless format so it will not lose quality again when you edit and save it.
