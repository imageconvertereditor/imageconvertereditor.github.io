How to convert PNG to JPG

  1. Tap "Choose a PNG image" and select a file from your phone.
  2. Move the quality slider if you want a smaller file.
  3. Tap "Convert to JPG".
  4. Tap "Download JPG Image" to save the new file.

Why convert PNG to JPG?

PNG files are often large. JPG files are much smaller, so they upload faster, take less space and are accepted by almost every website and form. Note that JPG does not support transparency, so any transparent area of your PNG will become white.
