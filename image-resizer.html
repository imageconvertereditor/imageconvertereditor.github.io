How to resize an image

  1. Tap "Choose an image" and select a photo from your phone.
  2. Enter the new width or height. Keep "Keep aspect ratio" ticked to avoid stretching.
  3. Pick JPG or PNG as the output format.
  4. Tap "Resize Image" and then "Download Resized Image".

Why resize images?

Many websites, forms and social media apps ask for a specific image size. Resizing also makes the file smaller, so it uploads and loads faster. Making an image much smaller reduces its detail, so keep the width as large as you can if quality matters.
