How to crop an image

  1. Tap "Choose an image" and select a photo from your phone.
  2. Pick a crop shape, or keep it on Free.
  3. Drag the box to the part you want. Drag the blue corner to make the box bigger or smaller.
  4. Tap "Crop Image", then tap "Download Cropped Image".

Why crop images?

Cropping removes the parts of a photo you do not need. It helps you focus on the main subject, fit profile pictures and social media posts, and reduce the file size.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. Cropping happens inside your browser, so your photos stay private.

Does cropping reduce image quality?

No. The cropped part keeps its original pixels. Only the area outside the box is removed.
